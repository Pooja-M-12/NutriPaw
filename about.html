About Nutri Paw

At Nutri Paw, we are passionate about improving pets’ health and happiness. Our vision is to become India’s most trusted pet wellness brand, offering tasty, affordable, and science-backed nutrition. Our mission is to provide premium supplements, treats, and wellness solutions to ensure your pets live long, happy lives.

Our Story

Founded by pet enthusiasts, Nutri Paw was created to bridge the gap between affordable and high-quality pet care. We focus on holistic pet wellness, combining grooming, nutrition, daycare, and boarding services. Every decision we make revolves around pets’ happiness and owners’ peace of mind.
